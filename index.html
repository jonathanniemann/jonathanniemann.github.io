About me

I am a postdoc in the Algebra group at the Technical University of Denmark (DTU), where I study algebraic curves and function fields over finite fields. I did my PhD under the supervision of Maria Montanucci and Peter Beelen, and I visited UNIMORE in Modena to work with Giovanni Zini. I received my BSc and MSc in Mathematics from the University of Copenhagen (KU) in 2020 and 2023 respectively, and during my studies I spent a semester at ETH Zürich.
